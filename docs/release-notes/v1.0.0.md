The first release of NotchKit, a SwiftUI app that turns your MacBook's notch into a home for widgets.

## Highlights

- **Widgets in your notch:** media player, calendar, Pomodoro, notes, to-dos, quick apps, shortcuts and a camera mirror. The notch grows to fit the widgets you pick.
- **Live activities:** while the notch is closed, it shows the track that's playing, the Pomodoro countdown, charging status, your next event and tray files.
- **Tray:** drop files onto the notch to keep them close, or AirDrop them in one move.
- **Gestures:** hover, click or swipe down to open, and swipe sideways to change tracks.
- **English and Turkish**, following your Mac's language by default.

## Install

1. Download **NotchKit-1.0.0.dmg** below and open it.
2. Drag **NotchKit** to **Applications**.
3. Open NotchKit. macOS blocks the first launch because the app isn't notarized yet: go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway** next to the NotchKit message.

Requires macOS 14.6 or later. Runs natively on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

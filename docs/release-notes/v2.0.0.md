## What's new

NotchKit 2 is rebuilt on [Sapphire](https://github.com/cshariq/Sapphire). The notch morphs smoothly between states, and there are many more widgets, live activities and system tools. Nothing in the app needs a paid service or account.

- **Widgets:** Music with synced lyrics, Weather, Calendar, Developer, Focus, Pomodoro, To-dos, Quick Apps, Timer, Notes, Clipboard, Shortcuts, Mirror and Battery
- **Developer panel:** follow AI coding agents, builds and dev servers, check your AI tools' usage and run commands in a built-in terminal
- **Home bubble:** while a live activity shows in the closed notch, click the bubble beside it to open your widgets
- **Proximity unlock:** lock the Mac when your phone walks away and unlock it when it comes back, with range calibration
- **System tools:** Snap Zones, HUD, clipboard history, Caffeinate, Eye Break, File Shelf with AirDrop, lock screen widgets, menu bar management and Launchpad
- **Languages:** English and Turkish, with a language picker in **Settings → General**

Upgrading from NotchKit 1.x keeps your to-dos, pinned Quick Apps and Pomodoro durations.

## Install

1. Download **NotchKit-2.0.0.dmg** below and open it.
2. Drag **NotchKit** to **Applications** (replace the old version if asked).
3. Open NotchKit. macOS blocks the first launch because the app isn't notarized yet: go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway** next to the NotchKit message.

Requires macOS 14.6 or later. Runs natively on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
